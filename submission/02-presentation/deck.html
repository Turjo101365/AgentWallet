The problem

Give an AI a wallet, and it can lose the money.

Hallucination

The model invents a recipient or an amount, and nobody notices before it's signed.

Prompt injection

Text hidden in a label, memo or web page redirects the payment.

Runaway autonomy

A scheduled agent keeps spending after conditions change, with no owner in the loop.

Asking the model to be careful is not a control. The controls have to sit outside the model.

AgentWallet02
The solution · four independent layers

Every payment crosses four gates.

1 · AI plans

gpt-4.1-mini turns plain English into typed tool calls. It has no tool that can sign, approve or loosen.

2 · Policy checks

14 block-pinned checks, including a pre-flight simulation, in trusted code on every path (15 for task runs).

3 · Owner authorizes

EIP-712 signature per transfer, or one bounded task grant with an auto-approval limit.

4 · Contract enforces

Allowlist, per-tx and daily caps, one-time intents, pause and emergency stop, on-chain.

Authorization

One signature per transfer, or one grant for many.

Owner approval · EIP-712 ApproveTransfer

Signs the exact proposal ID, intent ID, token, recipient, amount and deadline. Policy is re-checked at approval and again before broadcast.

public: 0x2c83a855…2e37fb · block 11926668

Bounded grant · EIP-712 AuthorizeTask

Fixes recipient, amount per run, max runs, max total, validity and schedule. Runs execute without a signature only under the owner's auto-approval limit; otherwise they escalate.

public: 0xedeb6b97…e3cec3 · block 11926686

Public signing used a test-only EIP-1193 wallet bridge. Real MetaMask 13.52.0 signing was verified on a local chain (clip 12).

Honest limits · roadmap

What we don't claim.

Limits

  • Public signing used a test wallet bridge; MetaMask is local
  • ETH only on Sepolia; ERC-20 and USD valuation are local (synthetic feeds)
  • RL timing is local and synthetic-trained
  • Audit chain is off-chain (hash-chained, not anchored)
  • Self-assessment only: no external audit

Next

  • Policy-bounded rebalancing: AI proposes, owner signs, using the executeSwap already deployed (not built yet)
  • Anchor the audit head on-chain
  • Multisig or hardware-wallet owner
  • Recurring autonomy on a public chain
